## Batch summary

Dominant failure class: **the empty-Δ product failures are driven by edit-resolution + evidence-capture, not by wrong diagnoses.** All four genuine edit-scenario fails (audio-transcribes, audio-tts, animatediff-vae, florence) share one shape: the agent produced a correct, graph-justified diagnosis and attempted the right edit; the editor rejected it on name/field resolution (`_resolve.py:1447` "Unknown graph name … Render the session again"; `unknown_target`/`unknown_field`/`unknown_port` for guessed names and `uid:`/numeric addresses; `invalid_arguments` for positional `widget_N` refs at `edit_tools.py:602-607`), the two-strike gate then locked further edits (`edit_tools.py:581-587`), and the agent submitted `no_change` with `route_not_applyable` (`_frag_response_contract.py:1210-1212`). Schema absence for custom-node classes (Apply Whisper, IndexTTS, QwenEmotion) made named-field discovery impossible, and Hivemind research was crippled by 57014 timeouts. On top of that, the judge's evidence surface is systematically stale: responses carry no `accepted_batch` envelope (`intent_judge.py:411-446` → "checked 0") and `final.ui.json` is byte-identical to `original.ui.json` in all six scenarios even where session logs show accepted deltas (audio-tts d1; d20410 turn-24 d1; florence turn-30 d1) — the judge is grading an artifact that cannot see landed edits (philosophy #1 corollary). Two of the six digest entries (caae97, 17dc9b) are fabricated pass→fail reversals whose quoted rationales contradict both the reply text and the terminal `assessment.json`; the digest itself needs an audit. Finally, one scenario (florence attempt_1) is pure infra (`timeout_or_infra`, killed before first attempt).

Highest-leverage fix: **make the emitted evidence surface reflect the durable session's final state — (1) always carry the accepted Δ (ops with landed status) in the response's `accepted_batch` envelope and re-emit `final.ui.json` from the retained IR after apply, so the judge's diff/replay can actually see a landed edit; and (2) fix first-touch name resolution by binding graph names at session load (or auto-refresh the name registry on `unknown_graph_name` instead of hard-rejecting and consuming the replacement window), plus a schema-less fallback that maps positional `widget_N` to the node's raw widget vector so custom nodes without local schema remain editable.** Fixing (2) converts the four edit fails' agent effort into landed Δs; fixing (1) makes the judge's verdict track reality. Secondary: either hydrate local schemas for the custom packs in the corpus (Apply Whisper, IndexTTS, QwenEmotion) or degrade `node_schema` no_results into a widget-vector fallback, and treat `unknown_graph_name` as a retryable render-refresh, not a semantic rejection.
