## Batch summary

Of the 4 scenarios actually covered by the batch-4 digest (brief said 6 — the other two are `unknown_needs_human`), the dominant failure class is **judge_or_harness_bug on the evidence surface**: in `multi-image-to-video-generation-with` the agent's correct KSampler edit (steps 30, dpmpp_2m) was **accepted as delta d1** and is present in the retained IR, yet the judged `response.json`/`final.ui.json` froze at the earlier "no change" submit, so the judge — correctly, on the artifacts it was given — failed an empty Δ; the post-fix re-assessment flips it to a full pass. In `multi-svd-…-webp-and-png` the same class of empty-IR resolver door from the batch-2 findings rejected a correct-intent `ImageFromBatch` insert (schema `available=True` three times), and even post-fix the landed node is orphaned. The remaining two are genuine model-capability failures, unchanged by any harness fix: `multi-ai-video-upscaling` produced uncited causal claims about unlabeled widgets (`widget_6='lab'`) and was correctly blocked by the grounding guard (grounding_or_evidence), and `multi-crops-face-previews` held a correct diagnosis but submitted a no-op clarify instead of a graph-local edit (refusal_or_block, philosophy #5's named worst outcome). **Single highest-leverage fix:** make the terminal judged artifact the *session's final state* — persist/refresh `response.json` and `final.ui.json` from the last accepted delta + last submit (carrying `accepted_delta_ids` through the replacement path into `_route_projects_final_from_original`, `artifacts.py:425`), so a landed edit can never again be invisible to the judge; that alone converts the one proven-pass-in-disguise scenario and closes the loop on the batch-2 projection fix. Secondary: teach the answer surface to cite `evidence_ids` on causal claims and to prefer a sanctioned `needs_input` clarify or a same-class parameter edit over a plain-text no-op.
