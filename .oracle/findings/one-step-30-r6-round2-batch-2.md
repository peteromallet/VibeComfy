## Batch summary

Dominant failure class: **judge_or_harness_bug via the response-projection defect** — 2 of 6 scenarios (chatterbox, animatediff-vae) are cases where the agent's edit was **correct and landed in the retained IR** (SaveAudio swap; batch_size 16→8), yet the judge's comparison surface (`final.ui.json` vs `original.ui.json`) was byte-identical because the r5→r6 accepted-delta guard is inert: the two_step executor nests `accepted_delta_ids` under `report.executor.execute`, while `_accepted_delta_ids()` (`agent/artifacts.py:339–346`) reads only the top-level envelope, so `_route_projects_final_from_original()` still projects final=original and the fail-closed guard (artifacts.py:433–436) never fires. The single highest-leverage fix: surface `accepted_delta_ids` at the top level of the two_step response envelope (or make `_accepted_delta_ids()`/`_route_projects_final_from_original()` read `report.executor.execute.accepted_delta_ids`), and derive `graph_unchanged` from the accepted-Δ ledger rather than the agent's prose — the retained IR is the one authority (philosophy #1/#2), and the judge must be able to see a landed edit. That one change flips 2 of these 6 fails outright and makes the indextts partial edit visible too; the remaining failures are budget design (transcribes/dual-checkpoint: research shares the 64-continuation budget and re-issued windows re-research from scratch — decouple/raise the research budget or persist collected results), the new P2 grounding gate regressing 17dc9b (contracts.py:2911 — count `hivemind_search` fetches as fetched-doc evidence or scope the gate to *recommendations*, not observed values), and the schema-unresolved-node dead-end for Apply Whisper (no named field exposed while positional refs are refused).
