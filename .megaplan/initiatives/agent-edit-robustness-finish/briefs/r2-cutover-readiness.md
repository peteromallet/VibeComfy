# R2 — Native Owner Cutover Readiness

## Outcome

Produce a current, executable, independently accepted cutover handoff: exact
consumer/deletion inventory, source-derived ledger rehearsal, faithful fault
harness, and real-ComfyUI incident reproduction—without routing a consumer,
retiring an owner, flipping a ledger row, or claiming M2 closure.

## Input handoff

- R1 accepted private executor, native-shape evidence, and initial cutover inventory.
- M1 contracts, predecessor authority ledger, and exact incident fixtures.
- The current production source at the R2 base SHA.

## IN

- Trace every native read/write consumer and every legacy definition, import,
  export, call, fallback, compatibility shim, and scanner exception that R3 must
  route or delete.
- Produce a machine-readable, base-SHA-bound cutover manifest with atomic edit
  groups, ownership invariants, static searches, and rollback instructions.
- Rehearse the source-derived ledger transformation in a detached artifact:
  expected moved tuples, deleted tuples, final zero-debt constraints, and
  bidirectional scanner checks. Do not modify the production ledger.
- Build/complete the faithful fault harness needed to prove every meaningful
  primitive/sub-primitive prefix during the later production cut.
- Reproduce the full `a66422e…` and `eb45e…` cases through pinned real ComfyUI;
  record exact environment versions, pre-cut behavior, stable identities,
  native normalization, and deterministic expected evidence.
- Dry-run the proposed public receipt API, consumer routing, deletion set,
  forbidden-token searches, broad test commands, and decisive incident scenarios
  against a disposable/non-production projection of the source.
- Obtain two independent readiness acceptances.

## OUT

- No production consumer routing, public mutation publication, owner retirement,
  source-ledger flip, compatibility deletion, or M2 closure.
- No verifier/controller/recovery implementation.

## Locked decisions

- This is evidence and rehearsal only; 0/27 transferred and all seven S4 rows
  remain truthfully open in production.
- R3 is one indivisible change; R2 may not create a “mostly routed” intermediate.
- Full incident fixtures and real native serialization are mandatory.
- Any source drift invalidates the certified manifest until regenerated.

## Open questions for the planner

- The exact post-cut ledger cardinality and target enums implied by current source.
- Whether any traced consumer appears to require forward replacement; prove it
  unnecessary or explicitly block readiness.
- Which fault prefixes and environment details are required to make R3 proof
  deterministic rather than best-effort.

## Constraints

- All rehearsal artifacts are detached; production modules and ownership ledger
  retain their current owners.
- Preserve protected files and accepted gates.
- No mock-only or reduced-fixture readiness claim.

## Done criteria

- Certified manifest covers every consumer and every deletion/dead-export target
  with no unclassified native path.
- Detached ledger rehearsal reaches 27/27 transferred and zero S3/S4 debt and is
  scanner-exact in both directions, while production ledger remains unchanged.
- Faithful harness covers all supported native shapes and partial prefixes.
- Both exact incidents reproduce deterministically in pinned real ComfyUI and
  expected post-cut assertions are machine-readable.
- Dry-run completes every routing/deletion/search/test step without ambiguity.
- Two independent acceptances declare R3 safe to execute; no production owner moved.

## Touchpoints

R1 core/tests, current consumer modules, authority scanner/ledger generator,
real-ComfyUI launcher, incident fixtures, disposable rehearsal artifacts, and
the new proof directory.

## Anti-scope

No production routing, deletion, ledger edit, M2 closure, feature flag,
compatibility facade, reduced fixture, self-attested native evidence, or
protected-file change.

## Output handoff and proof artifacts

- Base-SHA-bound `r2-certified-cutover-manifest.json`.
- Detached `r2-ledger-rehearsal.json` and exact scanner transcript.
- Faithful fault-harness evidence and `r2-real-fixture-reproduction.json`.
- Independent readiness acceptance explicitly authorizing R3 and nothing else.
